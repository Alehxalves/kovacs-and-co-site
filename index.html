Kovacs & Co

A private production tool for one YouTube channel.

Kovacs & Co is a personal, non-commercial tool used by a single person to produce English-language history videos and publish them to their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and no one else can log in to it.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads videos the owner produced to the owner's channel, scheduled as private until a set publish time.
  • Sets the thumbnail, captions, title and description of those videos.
  • Reads analytics for the owner's own channel.

See the privacy policy for the data it accesses and how to revoke access.
